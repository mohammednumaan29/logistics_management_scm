EVIDENCE-BASED GUIDANCE

AI Risk Insights

Risk factors are computed by the backend risk engine. Natural-language explanations come from the AI service when it is reachable, with deterministic backend fallback otherwise.

Explanations are grounded in recorded shipment and inventory fields. When the AI service is offline, backend rule-based explanations are labeled as fallback; unknown supplier lead times, alternate stock, or commercial impacts are never assumed.

About this workspace

DisruptionIQ Operations Analyst uses the authenticated Spring Boot backend for shipments, inventory, risk assessments, impact analysis, simulations, recovery plans, notifications, and audit activity. Display preferences and saved simulation notes stay in this browser; the workspace does not connect to a live warehouse, carrier, or ERP system.
